FAQs

How do I place a commission?

Send me a message on Twitter or by email with your references and what you have in mind.

What kinds of arts do you offer?

Sketched, coloured, and rendered pieces, plus chibified icons and additional characters. Fanart illustrations, Yumeships, and YCH inserts are also available.

Can I request changes?

Small adjustments can be discussed while the piece is in progress. Message me and we will work it out.

  3. Confirm the details and send me proof of payment.
